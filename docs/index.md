# Welcome to the Dentalemon guide

Dentalemon is a dental clinic app built for the iPad. The tooth chart is at the centre of every visit: you chart, plan, bill and book in one place.

This guide walks you through the everyday tasks, one page per task, with a picture of every step.

## How to use this guide

- **New to Dentalemon?** Read the pages in order. Each one builds on the last.
- **Looking for one thing?** Use the search box at the top, or pick a page below.
- **Words in bold** are exactly what you'll see on screen: a button, a tab or a menu item.
- **"You should see"** lines tell you what the screen looks like when a step worked.
- Tap any picture to see it full size.

!!! note "About the pictures"
    Every patient, phone number and ID in the pictures is made up for this guide. The screens were taken on an iPad-sized screen in landscape, so yours may look slightly different on a phone or in portrait.

## What Dentalemon can't do yet

We'd rather tell you now than have you look for it:

- **Insurance claims** are tracked by hand. You mark a claim submitted or paid yourself. Dentalemon doesn't send claims to PhilHealth or HMOs.
- **Sending an invoice or receipt** opens your iPad's Mail or Messages app. Dentalemon doesn't send it for you.
- **Orders**: you can add lab and imaging orders. Other order types can be read on older visits but not created.
- **Finished records stay final.** Undoing **End encounter**, or reopening a visit, doesn't unlock records that were already finalized. Use **Amend…** to correct one.
- **Users and access** in Settings is a preview. It doesn't yet change who can sign in or what they can do.

Something not working the way this guide says? Tell your Dentalemon contact, and mention the page and step number.
