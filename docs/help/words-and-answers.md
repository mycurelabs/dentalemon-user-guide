# Words and answers

What the words in Dentalemon mean, and answers to questions beta users often ask.
{ .meta }

## Words you'll see

**Encounter**
: One visit by one patient. It holds that day's dental chart, notes, prescriptions, orders and charges. You start an encounter when the patient is seen, and finish it when they leave. See [Start an encounter](../visits/start-an-encounter.md).

**Active, Pending, Ended, Closed**
: An open visit shows as **Active** or **Pending**. A finished one shows as **Ended** or **Closed**, and is view only.

**Chart**
: The drawing of the patient's teeth. Each tooth is shown from three sides, with its number underneath. See [Chart teeth](../visits/chart-teeth.md).

**FDI numbering**
: The usual way of numbering teeth, with two digits: the first digit is the quarter of the mouth, the second is the tooth. For example, **36** is the lower left first molar. You can switch to Universal or Palmer numbering in **Settings › General › Tooth notation**.

**Condition**
: Something you find on a tooth, such as **Caries** or **Fracture or crack**.

**Treatment**
: The work that fixes a condition, such as **Composite restoration**.

**Existing, Planned, Done today, Declined**
: How a treatment is marked on the chart. **Existing** is work the patient already had, like an old filling. **Planned** is work you'll do later. **Done today** is work you did in this visit. **Declined** is work the patient said no to.

**Estimate**
: The total fee of all **Planned** work. It goes down as work is done or declined. See [Treatment plan and Estimate](../visits/treatment-plan-and-estimate.md).

**Treatment plan**
: All the planned, done and declined work for a patient, across visits, with the **Estimate**. It's on the patient's **Treatment plan** tab.

**Charges**
: The list of what this visit costs: the visit type and each piece of work done today. You choose which lines go on the bill. See [Finish the visit](../visits/finish-the-visit.md).

**Cashier**
: Where the bill is paid. Charges are decided in the encounter; money is taken at the **Cashier**. See [Charges and the Cashier](../payments/charges-and-cashier.md).

**Invoice**
: The bill for a visit or a sale. It gets its own number once it's issued.

**Official receipt**
: The receipt for a payment. Its number is the next in your clinic's series.

**Coverage**
: The part of a bill that an HMO or PhilHealth pays instead of the patient.

**Payments due**
: Money that **patients** still owe. It's a tab in **Billing**.

**Accounts receivable**
: Money that **HMOs and PhilHealth** owe the clinic for covered work. It's a tab in **Billing**.

**Claim**
: The amount an HMO or PhilHealth owes for one bill. You mark it **Submitted** and **Paid** yourself; Dentalemon doesn't send claims for you.

**Draft and Requested orders**
: A new lab or imaging order starts as **Draft**. When you send it, it becomes **Requested**. Draft orders can also be sent when you finish the visit.

**Finalized record and Amend…**
: When a visit ends, its notes and records become final. To correct one later, open it and use **Amend…**.

**Archive**
: Takes a patient off your patient list without deleting their records.

**Sync**
: Keeping the app's data up to date with your clinic's data. The cloud icon at the top right shows the sync status.

## Common questions

### What does the cloud icon at the top mean?

It shows whether your data is up to date. A cloud with a tick means everything is saved and current. Tap it to see **Sync status**. If it says **You're offline**, check your Wi-Fi: changes you make can't reach the clinic until you're back online. If it says **Couldn't finish syncing**, tap **Try again**. See [Sign in and a first look](../getting-started/sign-in-and-home.md#7-check-the-icons-at-the-top-right).

### I saved something by mistake. Can I undo it?

Most changes show a message at the bottom with an **Undo** button for a few seconds. Tap it right away. After that:

- For a chart record, use the **⋯** menu on the record in the tooth panel.
- For a visit you ended, use **Reopen encounter** (today's visits only). Records that were finalized stay final; use **Amend…** to correct them.
- For an order you sent, use **Discontinue…** on the **Orders** page.

### I can't find a patient.

- Search by the last name, the first name or the patient number in **Patients**, or in the **Search…** box at the top of the sidebar.
- Check the spelling. Search also matches middle names, so a common name can bring up many patients.
- Archived patients don't appear in the list, and there's no list of archived patients in the app yet. Ask your Dentalemon contact if you need one back.

### How do I print?

- **A prescription**: in the encounter, open **Prescription** and tap **Print**.
- **An invoice or receipt**: open the invoice at the **Cashier** and tap **Print**.
- **A patient's records**: tap **Share records** at the top right of the patient's page, then choose **Print**. You can print the **Complete records** or the orders only.

Printing opens your iPad's print options, where you can also save as a PDF.

### Where did my follow-up booking go?

When you choose a **Follow-up** while finishing a visit, Dentalemon books it when you end the encounter. You'll find it in **Appointments** on the day and time shown in the follow-up card.

### Why is a button greyed out?

A greyed-out button is waiting for something. Usually a required box is still empty, or you haven't chosen anything yet. Look for a red message near the button, or a **Required** label in the list on the left. For example, **Add patient** stays greyed out until the name, sex and date of birth are filled in, and **Start encounter** is greyed out when the patient already has an open visit.

### Does Dentalemon send messages to patients?

No. Dentalemon doesn't send appointment reminders, confirmations or receipts by itself. **Send receipt** and **Send reminder** open your iPad's own apps, such as Mail or Messages, so you can send them yourself.

### Something doesn't work the way this guide says.

Tell your Dentalemon contact. Mention the page and step number, and what you saw on screen.
